Approach

The logo is the smallest part. The system is what the client keeps using.

A brand is a set of elements that work together: color, type, voice, imagery, layout, templates. When each one has a rule and a reason, anyone on the team can make something new and it still looks like the brand.

Listen

Discovery first

Workshops with founders and marketing leads to settle positioning, audience, and what the brand must never do, before a single sketch.

Reduce

Fewer, firmer decisions

A tight palette with defined roles, a fixed type scale, a small set of layout patterns. A system people can hold in their heads gets used.

Equip

Templates, not just rules

Guidelines written for marketers, with do/don't examples, plus decks, social, and vendor specs that produce on-brand work without a designer in the loop.

Review

Quality as a gate

Whether the asset comes from a teammate, an agency, or an AI tool, it passes the same check against the guidelines before it ships.

About

I'm Vanessa Flores, a creative director and brand designer in Los Angeles. In 2016 I founded Hook+Bridge, an independent studio where I run client relationships, set the creative direction, and stay hands-on from first concept to final production files. Today I lead a team of two designers; at its largest the studio ran with four designers and three marketing partners.

Before the studio I built the visual ecosystem for DecoBike, Florida's first public bike-share system, and led its creative through the transition to Citi Bike across every station and bicycle. At Medtronic I produced multilingual training and e-learning materials used across departments.

I work daily with Claude and Adobe Firefly for research, concepting, and production, with human review against brand guidelines as the quality gate. Currently also a design evaluator for AI training at Tastemaker, correcting generated brand and layout output against professional standards.

BFA, Graphic Design, Miami International University of Art and Design. Associate Degree, Instituto de Diseño de Caracas.
