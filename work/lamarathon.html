Los Angeles Marathon

An expansion of the Los Angeles Marathon identity into a full system for race season: a 41-page style guide, a library of LA-specific graphic elements, and the campaign that ran across print, out-of-home and digital.

  • Built the guidelines around elements runners already associate with the city: the Hollywood sign mountains, Walk of Fame stars and palm silhouettes, all redrawn as a reusable kit.
  • Defined colour roles, tints, apparel colours, typography and photography framing so vendors and the in-house team could produce on-brand work without a designer in the loop.
  • Rolled the system out to the billboard, print ads, web banners, email and stationery for the 2023 race.
