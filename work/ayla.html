Ayla Oasis

An ongoing brand system for Ayla, a seaside destination in Aqaba, Jordan, with residences, a marina, a golf club, hotels and beach clubs, and every venue and team that lives inside it.

  • Master brand guidelines plus separate print and digital media guides, so in-house teams and vendors produce on-brand work on their own.
  • A family of brochures in digital and print editions: destination, residential sales, golf club, marina village, berthing, weddings, sports, Feather Trail and OUM, plus destination maps.
  • Sub-brand identities with their own guidelines: B12 Beach Club (including a franchise deck), Ayla Golf Club, the AGC Hawks and Silica.
