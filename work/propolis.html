Propolis

Brand identity for Propolis, an urban co-living developer in Miami, from the logo to the buildings themselves.

  • A hexagonal mark that nods to the hive and to community living, with guidelines, merchandising and presentation templates.
  • Façade designs for Propolis properties: colour, supergraphics and paint plans applied to the architecture, from early options to final elevations and the finished buildings.
  • Leasing and investor materials: the website, pitch deck, Aerie opening campaign, interior signage, newsletters and a monthly social content package.
  • Art-directed the lifestyle photo shoot used across the brand.
